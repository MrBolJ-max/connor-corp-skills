# Objection Handbook — AI Sales Caller

Pre-built responses to 50+ common objections. The AI uses these patterns in real-time during calls.

## Price Objections

### "Too expensive"
**Pattern:** "too expensive", "can't afford", "high price", "costs too much"
**Response:** "I hear that. Let me ask — what are you spending on [current solution/SDR/marketing] right now? Most clients find this replaces $5-8K/month in payroll while doing 3× the volume."
**Follow-up:** "Would it make sense to see the ROI breakdown before deciding?"

### "We don't have budget"
**Pattern:** "no budget", "budget's tight", "cutting costs"
**Response:** "Totally understand. A lot of companies I talk to are in the same boat, which is exactly why they look at this — it usually *saves* money within 30 days. What's your current monthly spend on lead generation?"
**Follow-up:** "If I could show you how 3 clients cut their cost-per-lead in half, would that be worth a 10-minute call?"

### "Send me pricing"
**Pattern:** "send pricing", "email me the cost", "what's the price"
**Response:** "I'd rather show you exactly how it works for YOUR business first — pricing depends on call volume and features. Takes 15 minutes and you'll know if it's a fit. When's better, tomorrow or Thursday?"

## Timing Objections

### "Not right now"
**Pattern:** "not now", "bad timing", "busy season", "next quarter"
**Response:** "Fair enough — when WOULD be the right time? I can check back then, or better yet, send you a quick video now so you have all the info when you're ready."
**Follow-up:** "Most people who say 'not now' end up wishing they'd started 3 months earlier. Mind if I send the video just in case?"

### "Call me next month"
**Response:** "Absolutely — I'll mark it down. Quick question before I go: What specifically would change between now and next month that would make this a yes? Just so I'm prepared when I call back."

### "We're in the middle of [something]"
**Response:** "Got it — didn't mean to interrupt. Sounds like [initiative] is a priority. That's actually when a lot of our clients bring us in — [specific connection to their initiative]. Worth a 10-minute conversation once things settle?"

## Authority Objections

### "I'm not the decision maker"
**Pattern:** "not my decision", "need to ask", "not in charge"
**Response:** "Makes sense — who would be the right person to loop in? And is it okay if I send you both the info so you're aligned when you talk about it?"
**Follow-up:** "What's their main concern usually — cost, time to implement, or whether it'll actually work?"

### "I need to talk to my partner/boss/team"
**Response:** "Of course. What typically happens in that conversation — do they usually ask about cost, results, or implementation? I want to make sure I send you the right info to answer their questions."

## Product Objections

### "We already have [competitor]"
**Pattern:** "already use", "have a system", "using [tool]"
**Response:** "Nice — [competitor] is solid. What I'm hearing from people who switch is [specific pain point competitor doesn't solve]. Is that something you run into, or are you happy with how it's working?"
**Follow-up:** "If there was one thing you could change about [competitor], what would it be?"

### "We tried AI before and it didn't work"
**Response:** "Yeah, a lot of AI tools are garbage — bad scripts, robotic voices, no personalization. That's exactly why we built this differently. [specific differentiator]. Can I show you a 2-minute demo so you see the difference?"

### "We do this in-house"
**Response:** "That's great — means you already know the value. How many calls is your team making per day? What I'm finding is most in-house teams top out around [number] calls, and the follow-up falls through the cracks. Is that your experience?"

## Brush-Offs

### "Just send me an email"
**Response:** "I will — but honestly, 80% of emails I send get buried. This call is 2 minutes and you'll know if it's worth exploring. What do you actually look for in a [solution type]?"

### "Not interested"
**Pattern:** "not interested", "don't need", "not for us"
**Response:** "I get it — you're busy. Before I go, what does your current [lead gen/support/sales] process look like? Just curious what works for you."
**Secret weapon:** Often they'll start explaining their process, which opens the door.

### "I get calls like this all the time"
**Response:** "I bet — and most of them suck. That's why I said I'll keep this under 30 seconds. One question: Are you actually happy with your current [relevant metric], or is there room to improve?"

## Technical Objections

### "We don't have the tech team to implement"
**Response:** "That's the best part — we handle all implementation. You don't need a dev team. Usually takes 48 hours to go live, and we do the setup. Your team just watches the appointments roll in."

### "Sounds complicated"
**Response:** "It is complicated — on our end. For you, it's dead simple. [One-sentence explanation of user experience]. That's it. Want me to walk you through it in 5 minutes?"

## Trust Objections

### "How do I know this works?"
**Response:** "Smart question. Here's what I'd do in your position: [specific case study]. That was [timeframe] ago. They're now [result]. Want me to send you the case study + their contact info so you can verify?"

### "Sounds too good to be true"
**Response:** "It does — and honestly, it won't work for everyone. If you have zero leads or a terrible product, this can't fix that. But if you have a solid offer and just need more conversations, it works consistently. Can I show you the data?"

### "I've never heard of your company"
**Response:** "That's fair — we're newer and focused on [niche]. But our clients include [name drop if possible]. Would a reference call with one of them help?"

## Aggressive Responses (Use Sparingly)

### "Why are you calling me?"
**Response:** "Because I looked at {{lead_company}} and saw [specific observation]. I only call businesses I genuinely think this helps. What are you currently doing for [relevant process]?"

### "Remove me from your list"
**Response:** "Absolutely — done. Before I go, real quick: Is there anything we could have done differently that would've made this call useful, or are you just not in the market?"
