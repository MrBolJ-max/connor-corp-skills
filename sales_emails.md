**Halbert/Kennedy tactics baked in:**
- **Starving Crowd:** Targets service businesses already spending on growth
- **Price Anchoring:** $3,600–$6,000 vs. $497 creates instant value perception
- **You-Focused:** Every paragraph points to their time, margin, or calendar
- **Specificity:** Exact numbers, exact time commitment (5 minutes), exact mechanism
- **Soft CTA:** "Worth a demo?" / "Open to?" / "Want to see?" — low friction, easy no
- **Risk Reversal:** "No monthly bleeding," "once," "keeps the margin" — removes fear of subscription traps
