## What the Calls Sound Like

I spent 2 hours refining the system prompt so it doesn't sound like a robot.

It:
- Pauses naturally
- Says "um" occasionally
- Interrupts and gets interrupted
- Adjusts tone based on the lead's energy
- Knows when to stop talking

**Real transcript (anonymized):**

> **AI:** "Hi, this is Sarah from PostureBlend. Saw you were checking out our ergonomic chairs — did you find what you needed?"
>
